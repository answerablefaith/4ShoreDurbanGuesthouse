How to verify a Durban guesthouse booking and avoid scams

Published 21 August 2026 · Updated 21 August 2026 · 4Shore Guesthouse

A cheap-looking deal is not useful if you cannot confirm who is taking the booking. Before you pay for Durban accommodation, check the property, contact details and payment request through a trusted route.

Quick answer: Find the property's official website yourself, contact it through the details shown there, confirm your dates and final rate, and only then follow the agreed payment process. Treat unexpected links or changed bank details as a reason to check again.

Start from a source you trust

Durban Tourism warned visitors in February 2026 to contact accommodation providers directly through official details and to verify a business before paying. That is a useful first rule because scam messages can copy names, photos and booking language.

Instead of trusting the first message you receive, type the property's web address yourself or use a tourism listing you recognise. Compare the business name, address, phone number and email before moving to payment.

Use a five-check booking test

Before money changes hands, confirm five things:

  • Property: Is the name and address the place you meant to book?
  • Contact: Does the phone number or email match the official site?
  • Stay: Are the dates, guest count and room clear?
  • Price: Has the final rate been confirmed directly?
  • Payment: Does the payment method match what the property has just told you?

This takes a few minutes and gives you a clean point to stop if something changes. A new bank account, rushed payment request or unfamiliar link should trigger another direct check.

Do not let urgency replace verification

A real trip can still be time-sensitive. Rooms sell, plans change and guests sometimes book close to arrival. But urgency should not remove the basic checks.

Our view is simple: the more pressure a payment message creates, the more useful it is to leave that message and contact the property through a route you found independently.

Keep the booking details together

Save the agreed dates, rate, property address and contact person in one place. Keep the confirmation message or email. If anything later looks different, you have a clear record to compare.

FAQ: checking a Durban accommodation booking

How can I check if a Durban guesthouse booking is genuine?

Start from the property's official website, compare its details with a trusted tourism source, and contact the property directly before paying.

Should I pay from a link sent in a message?

Do not rely on an unexpected link alone. Go back to the property's official contact details and confirm the request directly.

What details should I confirm before paying?

Confirm the property name, address, dates, room, final rate, payment method and the person you are dealing with.
