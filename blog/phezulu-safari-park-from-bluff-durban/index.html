PheZulu Safari Park from the Bluff: what to book and when

Published 26 October 2026 · Updated 26 October 2026 · 4Shore Guesthouse

PheZulu gives you a taste of the Valley of a Thousand Hills, with wildlife, Zulu culture and reptile encounters. The best visit starts by choosing which part of the park suits your group.

Quick answer: PheZulu Safari Park is in Botha's Hill at 190 Old Main Road. Choose between a guided game drive, Zulu cultural show and reptile park. The operator lists opening hours of 08:00–16:30, but activity slots vary and drives depend on weather. Check PheZulu's site before you go.

Choose what you want to see

PheZulu Safari Park in Botha's Hill has three main paid experiences: a guided 4×4 game drive, Zulu dance and cultural shows, and a reptile park. The site also has places to eat and views over the Valley of a Thousand Hills. You don't have to buy every activity to enjoy the outing.

For us, the key decision is whether you want wildlife, culture or reptiles most. Pick that first, then fit a second activity around it if your group has the time and energy.

Know the game-drive limits

The operator lists one-hour game drives with set departures from 09:00 to 15:30. Daytime tickets are sold at the ticket office on a first-come basis, with at least two people per drive. All drives depend on the weather.

There is also a key health rule: the operator says these drives are not suitable for babies under six months, people in their third trimester of pregnancy or frail visitors because of the terrain. If this affects your group, choose an activity on foot and check access needs directly.

Match a cultural show to your day

PheZulu lists Zulu dancing and cultural shows at 10:00, 11:30, 14:00 and 15:30. The experience includes a look at customs and artefacts. It is a chance to learn, so arrive ready to listen rather than treating the performance as a photo stop.

We would choose a show slot before setting out. Then you can decide whether a reptile visit or a meal fits on the same trip. Check the day's show schedule when you arrive.

Decide whether the reptile park is enough

The reptile park features snakes, crocodiles, tortoises and a creature cave. It may suit a group that wants to learn about animals without taking a bumpy drive. Ask staff which areas are open and how tours work on the day.

If you are travelling with young children, let each child choose one creature to learn about. That's a good way to keep the visit focused without rushing past the displays.

Check the cost for your group

The operator's 2026 rates page lists adult game drives at R320, under-12 game drives at R220, adult cultural shows at R200 and under-12 shows at R140. The reptile park is listed at R75 for adults and R60 for under-12s. There is also a cultural-show and reptile-park combo ticket.

These are the operator's posted 2026 prices, not guesthouse rates. Check the latest prices and conditions on the day, especially for combos. Choose the experiences you will use rather than buying more just because you have made the trip.

Save the right address

Use PheZulu Safari Park, 190 Old Main Road, Botha's Hill, Outer West Durban, 3610. This is a trip away from the coast into the hills, not a quick walk from 4Shore. Look up your route and live traffic before departure.

The park lists hours of 08:00 to 16:30 most days, with closures for Christmas Day, the Amashova cycle race and the Comrades Marathon. Check its calendar for any changes. You can compare road and ride options in our Durban transport guide.

Have a choice if weather changes

Because game drives depend on weather, don't make a drive the only reason the day can work. Recheck conditions with PheZulu, then decide whether you still want the cultural show, reptile park or a meal.

That flexibility is more useful than trying to force the original plan. The official rates and times page and game-drive page are the best places to start. You can also ask the venue on +27 31 777 1000.

FAQs about PheZulu from the Bluff

Can I visit PheZulu Safari Park without taking a game drive?

Yes. The park offers cultural shows, a reptile park and places to eat. Check what is open when you plan to visit.

Do I need to book the daytime safari drive?

Daytime drive tickets are sold at the ticket office on a first-come basis, subject to weather and a minimum of two people. Sunset drives must be booked and paid for ahead of time.

Are the game drives suitable during pregnancy?

The operator advises that game drives are not suitable in the third trimester of pregnancy because of the terrain. Ask the operator about any other health or access needs.

Where is PheZulu Safari Park?

It is at 190 Old Main Road, Botha's Hill, Outer West Durban, 3610. Check your route from the Bluff before setting out.
