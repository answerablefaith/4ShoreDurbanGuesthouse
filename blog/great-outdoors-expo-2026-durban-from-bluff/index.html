Great Outdoors Expo 2026 in Durban: what to see before you buy

Published 30 October 2026 · Updated 30 October 2026 · 4Shore Guesthouse

Going to the Great Outdoors Expo in Durban? You can explore camping gear, 4×4 vehicles and outdoor ideas without spending the whole weekend there. Here's how to choose what matters to you.

Quick answer: The Great Outdoors Expo takes place on 14–15 November 2026 at Durban Exhibition Centre, 11 Walnut Road. The organiser lists adult entry at R100, with other ticket categories. Check gate hours and ticket terms on the official site before you leave the Bluff.

Start with the products you came to compare

The organiser groups its show around 4×4 vehicles, camping gear, outdoor living, travel and greener technology. It also lists food and drink exhibitors. Those areas appeal to different visitors, so decide on your main interest before arriving.

Our suggestion is to choose two products or ideas to explore. You might compare camping setups and solar gear, or look at a vehicle and then talk to a travel operator. That gives the visit a purpose without turning it into a shopping sprint.

Check the date, venue and gate times

The Great Outdoors Expo runs on Saturday 14 and Sunday 15 November 2026 at the Durban Exhibition Centre, 11 Walnut Road. The organiser's exhibitor page lists Saturday from 09:00 to 19:00 and Sunday from 09:00 to 16:00.

An older organiser brochure shows a shorter Saturday end time, so check the latest visitor hours directly with the event organiser before setting off. This matters most if you plan to arrive later in the day.

Know which ticket you need

The organiser's current ticket page lists adult entry at R100, tickets for children over ten at R60 and pensioner tickets at R80. The published categories do not explain every age case, so ask the organiser about younger children before you buy.

Those are expo admission prices, not 4Shore Guesthouse rates. Check the current ticket checkout for fees and final terms. Do not assume that an exhibitor demonstration or extra activity is covered by the gate ticket.

Use the displays to make a better decision

If you are keen on camping equipment, ask how it packs away, what is included and what you must buy on top. For vehicles, ask about service needs and what comes as standard. For off-grid gear, ask how the setup works when power use rises.

We think the value of a show like this is being able to compare claims face to face. Save notes and take product details home. You don't need to decide on a large purchase while standing at a busy stall.

Plan a family visit around energy, not every stand

A child who loves cars may want to spend most of the outing near the vehicle displays. Someone else may enjoy outdoor gear or food stalls more. Agree on one must-see area each before you enter.

Choose a meeting point if your group splits up, and check current venue rules for bags, access and facilities. If a demonstration draws a crowd, you can return later instead of pushing through.

Travel from the Bluff with a return plan

Use the Durban Exhibition Centre address in your map rather than looking for Durban ICC alone. The event is at the Exhibition Centre, and the same broad city area has several venues. Check traffic and parking rules on the day.

If you prefer a booked ride, save your pickup point before you enter. The 4Shore transport guide can help you weigh driving against other options. Do not rely on an unverified travel time.

Keep the rest of the day open

You could make the expo the main outing and return to the Bluff afterwards. If you spend longer comparing products than expected, you won't lose a paid booking elsewhere.

The event may be useful even if you buy nothing. A good question, a comparison and a few ideas for your next trip can be enough. For another way to spend time around your base, see things to do near the Bluff.

FAQ: Great Outdoors Expo 2026

When is the Great Outdoors Expo in Durban in 2026?

It is scheduled for 14 and 15 November 2026 at the Durban Exhibition Centre. Check the organiser for changes.

How much does entry cost?

The organiser lists R100 for adults, R60 for children over ten and R80 for pensioners. Check checkout fees and the rules for younger children.

Is the expo held at Durban ICC?

The published venue is Durban Exhibition Centre, 11 Walnut Road. Use that address when you plan your route.

Can I visit on Sunday?

Yes. The organiser's exhibitor page lists Sunday from 09:00 to 16:00. Confirm public opening hours before travelling.
