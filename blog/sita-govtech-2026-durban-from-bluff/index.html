SITA GovTech 2026 in Durban: a Bluff stay plan

Published 7 September 2026 · Updated 7 September 2026 · 4Shore Guesthouse

SITA GovTech 2026 is listed at Durban ICC from 21 to 23 September. If you are staying on the Bluff, a simple plan for each conference day can remove much of the travel guesswork.

Quick answer: Confirm your event programme, decide how you will travel to Durban ICC the night before, check live traffic in the morning, and leave spare time for registration or a busy arrival period.

Start with the official event dates

Durban ICC currently lists SITA GovTech 2026 for 21 to 23 September 2026. Treat the ICC listing and your own delegate information as the source for the event itself.

Do not build your day around an old social post or a third-party event summary. Conference programmes, access points and session times can change.

Plan the journey before conference morning

The Bluff gives you a separate base from the city centre. That can work well if you make the commute a planned part of the day rather than a last-minute decision.

Choose your transport the evening before. If you drive, check your event information for parking guidance. If you use a ride-hailing service or arranged transport, check availability before you need to leave. Our Durban transport guide explains the main choices.

Then check live traffic on the morning itself. A fixed travel-time promise is not useful in a city where traffic, incidents and events can alter a route.

Use a three-time conference plan

A useful way to plan is to work backwards from three times: when your first important session starts, when you want to reach the venue, and when you need to leave your accommodation.

Add time for registration, finding the right room and any security or entry process shown in your delegate information. If the first session is important, do not aim to arrive at the exact start time.

Keep the evening simple

Conference days can run longer than expected. Before you leave in the morning, decide whether you will eat near the venue, on the way back, or after you return to the Bluff.

This matters more on a multi-day event. A complicated evening plan can make the next early start harder. If you want to organise food around your stay, see our Bluff meal-planning guide.

Separate conference facts from accommodation facts

Your event organiser is the right source for registration, sessions and venue instructions. Your host is the right source for questions about your accommodation.
