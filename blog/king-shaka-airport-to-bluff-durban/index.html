King Shaka Airport to Bluff, Durban: an easy arrival plan

Published 24 July 2026 · Updated 26 July 2026 · 4Shore Guesthouse

Getting from King Shaka Airport to the Bluff is easier when you make three decisions before flying: how you will travel, which address you will use and when you will update your host.

Quick answer: Travel from King Shaka International Airport to the Bluff by pre-arranged transfer, accredited airport taxi, app-based ride or hire car. Save 28 Foreshore Drive, Ocean View, Bluff, Durban, 4052, confirm the collection point and message Winston after you collect your luggage.

Which transport option works best from King Shaka Airport to the Bluff?

A pre-arranged transfer works well when you want a named driver, a set meeting point and fewer decisions after the flight. It is often the clearest option for late arrivals, families, older travellers and groups carrying several bags.

An accredited airport taxi or app-based ride can suit a simple one-way journey. Airports Company South Africa lists taxi and shuttle services operating from King Shaka International Airport, so check the current official transport information and follow the signs in the arrivals area.

A hire car makes more sense when the airport transfer is only the first of several journeys. It may suit business guests with meetings across Durban or holiday guests planning day trips. Official car-rental desks operate at King Shaka International Airport, but availability and rates should be confirmed directly with the provider.

Our view: people often choose transport by looking at the first journey only. A better decision looks at the whole stay. Paying for a hire car that stays parked for two days can be less useful than arranging one reliable transfer and booking other trips as needed.

How should you compare a taxi, transfer and hire car?

We think the best transport choice is the one that leaves the fewest unanswered questions. Price matters, but so do luggage space, collection instructions, payment method and what happens if the flight is delayed.

What details should you save before the flight?

Use the full address: 28 Foreshore Drive, Ocean View, Bluff, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, 4052. Save it in your phone, booking notes and a screenshot. Do not rely on typing only “the Bluff” or “Anstey’s Beach” into a driver’s app after landing.

Before entering a vehicle, confirm the driver name, vehicle details, destination and payment method. Follow current airport signs because collection areas can change. The airport information desk can also direct passengers when the meeting point is unclear.

When should you tell 4Shore Guesthouse that you are arriving?

Winston is the owner and host of 4Shore Guesthouse. Guests can contact him about rooms, rates, availability and practical arrival questions. Send him your expected landing time before travelling, but do not present that as your exact check-in time.

After collecting your bags, send a short update. A message such as “We have our luggage and are meeting the driver now” is more useful than a precise arrival estimate made before the flight. If the flight is delayed, update both Winston and the transport provider as soon as the change is clear.

Our opinion is that arrival communication should be simple, not constant. One message before the trip, one after baggage collection and one only if something changes is usually enough to keep everyone informed without turning the journey into a running commentary.

What should stay in your hand luggage?

Keep your phone, charger, address, booking details, transport confirmation and essential medicine within reach. Do not pack every arrival item in a checked suitcase.

If you hire a car, check the boot space before leaving the rental area. If you book a taxi or ride, select a vehicle size that matches the number of travellers and bags. A vehicle that technically carries four people may not hold four large suitcases comfortably.

How should you plan the first evening on the Bluff?

Keep the first evening small. Check in, settle your luggage and confirm food or essential shopping before travelling if you expect to arrive late. Do not assume every nearby business will still be open.

4Shore Guesthouse is across Foreshore Drive from Anstey’s Beach. If you arrive after dark, save the beach for daylight. Our view is that the better first impression is often the one you do not force. Rest, organise the next morning and start the beach part of the stay when you can see the area properly.

FAQ: King Shaka Airport to the Bluff

How do I get from King Shaka Airport to the Bluff in Durban?

Should I arrange transport from King Shaka Airport before flying?

Arrange it in advance if you arrive late, travel with children, carry several bags or prefer a named driver and meeting point. A daytime solo traveller with light luggage may be comfortable choosing a taxi or app-based ride after landing.

What address should I use for 4Shore Guesthouse?

Use 28 Foreshore Drive, Ocean View, Bluff, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, 4052. Send your expected arrival time to Winston, the owner and host, before travelling.

Should I hire a car for a stay on the Bluff?

A hire car is useful when you plan several Durban trips, meetings or day outings. For a short stay centred on Anstey’s Beach, a direct airport transfer may be simpler and avoid paying for a car you use very little.
