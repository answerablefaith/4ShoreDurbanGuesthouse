King Shaka Airport to Bluff, Durban: an easy arrival plan

Published 24 July 2026 · 4Shore Guesthouse

A smooth Durban stay starts before you leave the airport. Decide how you will travel, share the full address and keep your host updated if your flight changes.

Quick answer: Choose a pre-arranged lift, airport taxi, app-based ride or hire car. Confirm the collection point and destination before you set off. For 4Shore Guesthouse, use 28 Foreshore Drive, Ocean View, Bluff, Durban, 4052, and send Winston your expected arrival time.

King Shaka International Airport sits north of central Durban, while the Bluff is on the southern side of the city. The simplest plan is to treat the airport journey as a separate part of your trip. Sort it before you fly rather than making every decision beside the baggage carousel.

Choose the right arrival option

A pre-arranged lift gives you a named driver and meeting plan. An airport taxi or app-based ride can suit a simple one-way arrival. A hire car may work better when you plan several Durban outings or day trips during your stay.

There is no single best choice for every guest. Think about your landing time, luggage, group size and plans after check-in. Families may value fewer handovers. Solo travellers may prefer a direct ride. Guests arriving for work may want a clear receipt and collection record.

Confirm the details before leaving the terminal

Use the full street address, not only “the Bluff” or “Anstey’s Beach”. Check the driver name, vehicle details, collection point and fare method before entering a car. Airport transport arrangements can change, so use the live airport signs and official collection areas when you land.

Keep your phone charged and connected. Save the guesthouse number and take a screenshot of the address. This gives you a backup if mobile data is slow or a map pin loads badly.

Tell the guesthouse when you expect to arrive

Winston is the owner and host of 4Shore Guesthouse. He is the person guests can contact directly about rooms, rates, availability and practical arrival questions. Send your flight arrival time, then update him if you face a long delay.

Do not use your scheduled landing time as your exact check-in time. You still need to leave the aircraft, collect luggage and meet your driver. A simple message after landing helps the guesthouse prepare for your actual arrival.

Make the first evening easy

After a flight, keep the first plan small. Check in, settle your luggage and confirm what you need for the evening. If you are hungry, ask about practical meal options before travelling. If you arrive late, do not assume every shop or restaurant will still be open.

4Shore Guesthouse is across Foreshore Drive from Anstey’s Beach. Save the beach for daylight if you arrive after dark. A short reset, a good sleep and a planned morning usually make a better start than trying to fit in too much.

Use a simple arrival checklist

  • Book or choose your airport travel option.
  • Save the full guesthouse address and phone number.
  • Confirm the collection point and driver details.
  • Send your expected arrival time to Winston.
  • Update him after landing if your timing changes.
  • Keep your first meal and first evening simple.
