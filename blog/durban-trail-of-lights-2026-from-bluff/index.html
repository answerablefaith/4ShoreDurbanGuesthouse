Durban Trail of Lights 2026: choose your evening at the Gardens

Published 9 November 2026 · Updated 9 November 2026 · 4Shore Guesthouse

Durban Botanic Gardens lights up again this summer. Its tenth Trail of Lights has new displays and two evening entry slots. Here's how to choose the night that suits your Bluff stay.

Quick answer: Trail of Lights is scheduled for 9 December 2026 to 2 January 2027 at Durban Botanic Gardens. Pick an 18:00 or 19:30 entry slot. The organiser lists tickets from R85 online and R120 at the gate. Check Durban Tourism's event notice and live ticket stock before leaving.

Choose a date and entry slot

The Trail of Lights runs from 9 December 2026 to 2 January 2027 at Durban Botanic Gardens in Berea. The announced entry slots are 18:00 and 19:30. Check the live calendar for your chosen night, as not every date may have the same ticket stock.

We would choose 18:00 for children who tire early. The 19:30 slot may suit adults who want to eat before leaving. This is a choice about your group, not a claim that one slot has better lights.

Know what is new for the tenth year

The 2026 theme is ‘Discover a New World’. The organiser has announced a Tree of Wonder and new themed displays. The familiar food gardens, live entertainment, Santa's Grotto and Santa's Village also return.

If your group likes activities, look for the listed zipline, virtual reality and Build-A-Bear pop-up. Check at the event whether these need their own booking or payment. An entry ticket should not be taken as proof that every add-on is included.

Work out the ticket cost before you go

The latest Durban Tourism announcement lists tickets from R85 per person through Webtickets. It quotes a gate price of R120 per person. Children aged two or under may enter free, and pensioner or group discounts are advertised.

These are event prices, not guesthouse rates. Check the final total and ticket type at checkout. We think booking the right time slot is worth more than arriving at the gate and hoping your preferred session is still open.

Travelling with a dog? Pick the right night

The event advertises Paws ’n Claus charity evenings on 10 and 14 December. Its published dog entry charge is R60 per dog. Those are special dates, not a sign that pets are welcome on every night.

Check the organiser's pet rules and your own transport before buying. If you are not taking a dog, you can choose another date and avoid the extra event conditions.

Keep the visit within your family's limits

The displays are outdoors, so check the forecast and the organiser's weather policy before setting out. Choose shoes for walking and ask about current step-free access if someone in your group needs it.

Children may want Santa's Grotto or the activity zone more than the full light trail. Pick one must-see stop each and leave room for a break. The two food gardens give you an option to eat on site; check prices and any rules on bringing food.

Get the right Durban Gardens address

The event is at Durban Botanic Gardens on John Zikhali Road, Berea. Do not confuse it with the Japanese Gardens in Durban North. The two sites are in different areas and serve different outings.

From 4Shore on the Bluff, check traffic and parking before leaving. Our Durban transport guide can help you weigh driving against a booked ride. Arrange your return before you enter the trail.

Why this is not a daytime garden walk

Our daytime Botanic Gardens guide covers a relaxed garden visit. Trail of Lights is a ticketed, timed evening event with new displays and separate entry rules.

For a December stay, you could keep the morning free and make this the main event after sunset. That gives you the energy to enjoy what you came to see, rather than fitting the lights between other paid stops.

FAQs: Trail of Lights 2026

When is Durban Trail of Lights 2026?

The announced season runs from 9 December 2026 to 2 January 2027 at Durban Botanic Gardens. Check the live ticket calendar for your date.

Are the 18:00 and 19:30 slots different shows?

They are listed entry times. The organiser has not said the displays differ by slot, so choose the time that suits your group.

Do children under three need tickets?

The published offer says children aged two and under enter free. Check the ticket site's rules when you book.

Can I take my dog on any night?

No general pet access is advertised. The announced dog-friendly charity nights are 10 and 14 December, with a listed charge per dog.
